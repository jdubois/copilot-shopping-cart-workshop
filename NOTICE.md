# Provenance

The shopping-cart exercise is authored for this website. It is not extracted from
BootUI's full sample reactor. All products, prices and carts are synthetic.

The three bundled Java skills are unmodified copies from `github/awesome-copilot`
at `7e408815ddcb64889a3683799a85399331cfd4ef`. Their MIT license is in
`.github/skills/UPSTREAM-LICENSE`; hashes and source revision are in
`.github/skills/provenance.json`. Required references are included.

The generated Maven wrapper scripts retain their Apache-2.0 notices.
Spring Boot, H2 and BootUI dependencies retain their respective upstream licenses;
they are downloaded by Maven, not repackaged into the source archives.
